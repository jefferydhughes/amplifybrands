01 — Foundation

Brand personality

Everything below this line should pass one test: would the smartest, slightly-tired-of-everyone's-BS person in the room sign off on it? If it smells like a PowerPoint, kill it.

▲

We are

Bold. Sharp. Slightly rebellious. High-contrast and fast-moving. Confident without the cosplay. Blunt, funny, honest, anti-corporate, practical.

✕

We are not

Cringe startup bros. Over-polished consultants. Motivational speakers. Robotic tech nerds. Buzzword addicts. No "SaaS dashboard template" energy.

◎

How it should feel

Relieved. Understood. Less overwhelmed. Cautiously excited. "Thank god — someone who can just do this for us." We're the calm adult in the AI circus.

05 — Foundation

Motion

Fast. Subtle. Responsive. Never gimmicky. Motion confirms an action or rewards a scroll — it never performs for its own sake. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Fast
120ms · ease-snap
hovers, presses, ticks
Base
200ms · ease-snap
color, border, transform
Slow
420ms · ease-out
scroll reveals, entrances
ease-snap
cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)
ease-out
cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)
The rule of thumb: if a visitor would notice the animation as an animation, it's too much. They should just feel that the site is fast and responsive. The headline typing in the hero, hover lifts on cards, the count-up stat — each does one job, then gets out of the way.

07 — Experience

The fun layer

"Fun on arrival" without breaking the "never gimmicky" rule. Fun lives in copy, contrast, and a few sharp kinetic moments — not in confetti. Here's exactly where the personality is allowed to show up, and where it isn't.

Hero · on load
Typed headline reveal
"We Make AI ___ Easy" types in with a blinking cursor, lands on "Stupidly," then settles. One time, ~1.2s, then still. Sets tone in the first second.
Scroll · reward
Count-up stats
"14 hrs saved / week" counts up when it scrolls into view. Proof that feels earned, not claimed. Fires once.
Hover · feedback
Card lift + lime focus
Cards rise 4px and sharpen their border. Primary buttons throw a lime glow. Fast, physical, satisfying.
Copy · everywhere
Pattern-interrupt voice
"Cool. Cool cool cool." / "Revolutionary concept, we know." The biggest source of fun is words, not motion. Free, and never gimmicky.
Micro · delight
One small easter egg
The logo dot is pokeable — click it and it pulses lime. That's the ceiling. One wink, not a circus.
Accent · scarcity
Acid green as punctuation
Lime appears maybe 3–4 times per screen, max. Its rarity is what makes the site feel sharp instead of loud.
Hard limits (the "never gimmicky" guardrails): no autoplay video backgrounds, no parallax that fights scrolling, no spinning 3D logos, no confetti, no cursor trails, no sound. If reduced-motion is on, every effect above degrades to a clean static state. Fun should make people grin, not reach for the back button.
08 — Direction

Imagery & iconography

The fastest way to look like every other AI agency is stock futurism. We go the other way: brutal typography and real systems over fake robots.

◉ Use
  • Brutal, oversized typography as the hero image
  • Bold one-line statements on near-black
  • Real workflow diagrams & product screenshots
  • Gritty textures, sharp grids, abstract systems visuals
  • Kinetic motion that shows a process working
✕ Avoid
  • Robots, androids, glowing brains
  • Holograms and fake "future" UI graphics
  • Stock people pointing at screens
  • Neon-sign-and-fake-Lamborghini guru energy
  • Anything that smells like a beige boardroom
Icons: thin-to-medium line weight, geometric, monochrome (white or lime). No gradients inside icons. When in doubt, a sharp glyph beats an illustration.

Vocabulary. Lean on: useful · practical · systems · automate · bottlenecks · implementation · chaos. Never ship: synergy · disruptive · transformative · paradigm · ecosystem · innovation · next-generation. Profanity is allowed but controlled — a scalpel, not a fire hose.

Fonts

Load Space Grotesk (400–700) + Inter (400–700) from Google Fonts. Self-host for production speed. System-sans fallbacks already in the stack.

Theme

Dark-first and dark-only. There is no light mode — near-black is the brand. Don't invert it.

Accent discipline

One primary (lime) action per viewport. If two things are lime, nothing is. Violet is decoration, never a CTA.

Motion baseline

Ship every effect with a prefers-reduced-motion fallback. Animations enhance; they're never required to use the site.

Layout

1180px max container, 24px gutters, generous vertical rhythm (--section-y). Whitespace is the design.

Copy is a component

Don't let placeholder lorem ship. Pull real lines from the voice section — the words carry as much of the brand as the pixels.
