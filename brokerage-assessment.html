The real problem

Most brokerages aren't struggling because they lack leads. They're struggling because they can't fully capitalize on the leads they already have.

A lead sits untouched for 30 minutes. An agent forgets a follow-up. Data gets entered twice. A coordinator spends hours chasing information that already exists somewhere else.

Individually, these don't feel expensive.

Collectively, they're costing brokerages tens or hundreds of thousands per year.
