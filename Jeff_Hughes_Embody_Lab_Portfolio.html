01 · Scale

Building the marketing engine for a global online education brand

Skill Samurai — Founder & CEO

An enrichment education brand I built from zero to a multinational operation serving over 250,000 students across seven countries. Through the growth phase I personally owned every layer of the marketing function.

  • The full acquisition stack. Google Ads, Meta ads, PR campaigns, influencer strategy, and long-form content marketing — every channel measured against booked trials and enrolled students, not vanity metrics.
  • Segmented funnels for structurally different audiences. B2B franchise buyers lived on LinkedIn. B2C mom-of-student buyers lived on Facebook. Same brand, entirely different messaging architectures, creative, and conversion mechanics. I built and ran both concurrently — the same discipline required when one platform serves clinicians, coaches, and consumers on structurally different intents.
  • Influencer program built at ground level. Recruited 36 influential mom bloggers through a wine-and-cheese info-night model — free enrollment for their child, plus a ticket to give away. Low CAC, exceptionally high referral quality.
  • Book as a top-of-funnel asset. Wrote Raising Smarter Kids with AI as a marketing anchor, then hired a publicist. Multiple television appearances as a subject-matter expert followed. The same earned-media lever leading practitioners in healing and education already use — a playbook I've run end-to-end.
  • International localization. Marketing sites, ad creative, and messaging built market-by-market — not translated as an afterthought.
  • COVID pivot. Moved the entire global operation from in-person to online delivery and developed new interactive tutoring techniques inside the platform to hold engagement and retention.

02 · Cohort & Certificate Programs

Designing and launching online cohort programs end-to-end

Kingswood University Online MBA

As Head of the Business Department, I designed and launched Kingswood's online MBA program end-to-end. The first cohort launched May 2026. I set the program outcomes, wrote the course outlines, hired the faculty and instructional designers, and led the go-to-market. Cohort model, live faculty sessions, asynchronous supplements, launch marketing — the closest structural analog in my history to a live online certificate program.

Skill Samurai University

A 40-hour online training program I designed to certify new franchisees. Delivered to cohorts across multiple countries with assessment, retention mechanics, and gamification built in. The playbook we used to keep completion rates high across international cohorts — including time-zone-friendly cadence and community-based accountability — translates directly to any 60-hour live online certificate.

Virtual private school — client engagement

Full-funnel build for a client launching a virtual K–12 school. Landing pages, ad copy, evergreen webinars, booking pages, curriculum architecture, and the website itself. Shipped end-to-end.
