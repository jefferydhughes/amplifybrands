# DESIGN SYSTEM — AMPLIFY BRANDS AI

## Brand Personality

The visual identity should feel:
- bold
- sharp
- slightly rebellious
- modern
- high-contrast
- fast-moving
- anti-corporate

Think:
"premium startup operator"
NOT:
"SaaS dashboard template"

---

# Color Palette

## Primary Background
#0B0B0B

Near-black.
Makes everything feel premium.

---

## Primary Text
#F5F5F5

Soft white.
Never pure white.

---

## Accent Color
#A3FF12

Acid green / electric lime.

Aggressive.
Modern.
Attention-grabbing.

Used sparingly.

---

## Secondary Accent
#5E5E5E

Muted gray for supporting text.

---

## Optional Highlight
#6B5BFF

Electric violet for gradients or hover states.

---

# Typography

## Headings
Use:
- Space Grotesk
OR
- Satoshi
OR
- Neue Montreal

Heavy weight.
Large scale.
Tight line-height.

---

## Body
Use:
- Inter
OR
- General Sans

Readable.
Clean.
Neutral.

---

# Layout Style

- Large bold headlines
- Massive whitespace
- High contrast
- Sharp grids
- Minimal clutter
- Fast scanning
- Sections that feel editorial

---

# UI Style

Buttons:
- chunky
- rounded slightly
- bold text
- high contrast

Cards:
- dark surfaces
- subtle borders
- mild blur/glow
- minimal decoration

Animations:
- fast
- subtle
- responsive
- never gimmicky

---

# Imagery Direction

Avoid:
- robots
- glowing brains
- stock people pointing at screens
- holograms
- fake “future” graphics

Use:
- brutal typography
- bold statements
- workflow diagrams
- screenshots
- gritty textures
- real UI
- kinetic motion
- abstract systems visuals

---

# Website Inspiration

Visual references:
- King Kong
- Linear
- Ramp
- Vercel
- Clay
- Contra

---

# Social Content Style

Content should:
- challenge assumptions
- expose bad workflows
- mock AI hype culture
- simplify complex ideas
- make operators feel understood

Tone:
"smart friend calling out obvious nonsense."

---

# CTA Philosophy

Never sound desperate.

CTAs should feel:
- confident
- direct
- low-friction

Examples:
- Book Your AI Breakdown
- Show Me The Bottlenecks
- Fix My Workflow
- Make AI Useful
