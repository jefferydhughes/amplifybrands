Fair questions

No magic beans. No bullshit.

Is Amplify an AI company?

No. We are a franchise growth and execution partner. We use practical tools behind the scenes when they reduce repetitive work, but technology is not the product. Getting useful work done is the point.

Does the founder have to make content all day?

No. The core input is one guided conversation a month. The founder brings the lived experience; Amplify turns that into a connected system.

Will you promise franchise sales?

No. Anyone who promises a franchise sale from a few months of content is guessing. We build the system, connect attention to meaningful actions, and use the evidence to improve it.

What gets measured?

The things that show whether the system is doing useful work: publishing consistency, content engagement, relevant page visits, calls to action and where people lose momentum.

What happens when we are ready to start?

We begin with a conversation about your growth priorities, current execution gaps and whether the system is the right fit. If it is, we map a practical first move. If not, we will say that too.
