## 🎯 Core asset

### Logo — "Pulse A"
- Geometric capital "A" outline (light `currentColor`) whose crossbar is replaced by
  **three ascending lime pulse bars**. This is the brand signature.
- Path (viewBox 0 0 120 120): `M 4 110 L 52 8 L 68 8 L 116 110 L 96 110 L 60 30 L 24 110 Z`
- Pulse bars (always lime `#A3FF12`, never violet):
  - `x=42 y=84 w=8 h=14`
  - `x=56 y=78 w=8 h=20`
  - `x=70 y=70 w=8 h=28`
- Usage in this film: the three pulse bars are the **persistent hero element** — they
  recur across scenes (sarcastic spinner → tangled "spaghetti" → clean workflow lines →
  showcase modules) and finally **converge/morph into the full Pulse A** at the close.
- Never distort/restretch/recolor the bars.

## 🎨 Auxiliary

### Palette (extracted from live CSS)
- Background:  `#0B0B0B`  (near-black, slight warmth)
- Ink:         `#F5F5F5`
- Accent/Lime: `#A3FF12`  (the organizing force — withheld until Scene 4)
- Lime hi:     `#C6FF6B` / `#8FE000` (glow / shade)
- Coral:       `#FF5C5C`  (chaos / error / "the noise we're against")
- Violet:      `#6B5BFF` / `#9A8FFF` (hype / guru-slop — used ONLY as satire, isolated)
- Mute grays:  `#A1A1A1` `#5E5E5E` `#242424` `#1C1C1C` `#161616` `#101010`

### Type
- Display: `'Space Grotesk', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif` (700) — kinetic headlines
- Body/UI: `'Inter', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`
- Mono (HUD/labels): `ui-monospace, 'SF Mono', monospace`

### Signature detail
- The 120%-polished moment: the **bars→logo converge** at the close.

### Tone keywords
- dark · fast · irreverent · operator-focused · "the calm adult in the AI circus"

## Anti-slop notes
- Violet gradients here are the *legitimate exception* — they exist to satirize AI
  hype/guru slop, isolated to Scenes 1/3/6, never the brand's own voice.
- Lime is rationed: it does NOT appear until "the shift" (Scene 4), so its arrival reads
  as order replacing chaos.
