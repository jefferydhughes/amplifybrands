# BRAND VOICE — AMPLIFY BRANDS AI

## Core Personality

Amplify Brands speaks like:
- the smartest person in the room
- who is also slightly tired of everyone’s bullshit

We are:
- blunt
- sharp
- funny
- honest
- anti-corporate
- practical
- confident

We are NOT:
- cringe startup bros
- overly polished consultants
- motivational speakers
- robotic tech nerds
- corporate buzzword addicts

---

# Tone Rules

## Use:
- short punchy sentences
- conversational rhythm
- unexpected humor
- controlled profanity
- brutal honesty
- strong opinions
- emotionally accurate observations

## Avoid:
- jargon
- corporate filler
- buzzwords
- sounding “inspirational”
- sounding fake
- sounding overly salesy

---

# Writing Style

Write like:
- a smart operator
- explaining things to another smart operator
- over coffee after both surviving a chaotic week

Sentence fragments are okay.

One-line paragraphs are encouraged.

Pattern interrupts are important.

Humor should feel observational, not try-hard.

---

# Examples

## BAD
"We leverage cutting-edge AI solutions to transform operational efficiencies."

## GOOD
"We use AI to eliminate repetitive work your team secretly hates."

---

## BAD
"Our innovative systems empower scalable synergy."

## GOOD
"Less manual work. Fewer bottlenecks. More getting shit done."
