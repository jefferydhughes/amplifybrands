Fair questions

No magic beans. No bullcrap.

Is Amplify an AI company?

No. We’re a founder content partner for franchisors. We use practical tools behind the scenes to transcribe, sort and draft, but a human edits everything and your team approves it. We never use AI to fake your founder’s face or voice.

Does the founder have to make content all day?

No. The core input is one guided conversation a month, on Zoom, from their desk. No studio, no script, no makeup.

Whose voice is it?

Your founder’s. Every post comes from something they actually said, written the way they talk. If it doesn’t sound like them, it doesn’t go out.

What about earnings claims?

Any post about what franchisees earn, spend or make back is checked against your Franchise Disclosure Document, under rules your franchise counsel sets with us in month one. Nothing that could read as a financial performance claim goes out unchecked.

Will you promise franchise sales?

No. Anyone who promises a franchise sale from a few months of content is guessing. We build the system, connect attention to real inquiries, and use the evidence to improve it.

What about our franchisees’ local social?

Founder first. Local content comes later, once the founder engine is working. Ask us about it in the audit.
